BENCHMARK TIMELINE

The Fluency Milestones You Can Expect

Measurable progress benchmarked every 4 to 6 weeks of dedicated cohort learning.

WEEK 1 – 4

Phonetics & Ease

Phonetic foundation established, fear of making mistakes eliminated, basic everyday questions answered comfortably.

WEEK 5 – 12

Sentence Spontaneity

Forming complex compound thoughts, holding 15-minute unscripted conversations, expressing personal opinions freely.

WEEK 13 – 20

Professional Debate

Leading discussions, comprehending native fast-paced podcasts, preparing formal written proposals and presentations.

WEEK 21 – 24+

Certified Mastery

Acing official CEFR examinations (IELTS, DELF, Goethe, DELE) and speaking with natural cultural poise.
