🇩🇪 GERMAN LANGUAGE CEFR Levels A1 – B2

German Professional & Higher Studies Track

A comprehensive linguistic masterclass designed for students and professionals seeking university admission in Germany or engineering and medical careers across DACH nations.

Enroll In This Program Check Available Batches
SYLLABUS BREAKDOWN

What You'll Learn

Six integrated competency pillars developed to guarantee holistic linguistic confidence.

1. Spontaneous Speaking

Real-world dialogue simulation, university seminar discussions, job interviews, and spontaneous everyday conversational ease.

2. Native Listening

Comprehend natural German radio broadcasts, university lectures, regional DACH accents, and fast-paced workplace dialogues.

3. Analytical Reading

Read academic articles, technical contracts, German news outlets (Der Spiegel, Die Zeit), and official administrative documentation.

4. Structured Writing

Compose formal German cover letters (Anschreiben), academic essays, professional emails, and structured argumentative texts.

5. Lexical Mastery

Acquire 3,500+ high-frequency words, compound noun logic, idiomatic expressions (Redewendungen), and sector-specific terminology.

6. Accent & Phonetics

Master the German Umlauts (ä, ö, ü), glottal stops, r-pronunciation, and melodic sentence intonation for clean clarity.

Detailed Module Curriculum

Alphabet & phonetics, basic verbs in present tense, nominative and accusative cases, personal introductions, shopping, restaurant ordering, asking for directions, and forming simple questions.
Modal verbs, dative case, prepositions with dative/accusative, talking about past events (Perfekt & Präteritum), making appointments, housing & rental contracts, and expressing preferences.
Genitive case, subclauses (weil, dass, obwohl, wenn), passive voice, conditional (Konjunktiv II), delivering presentations, writing professional emails, and comprehensive Goethe B1 exam simulation.
Complex sentence connectors, nuanced idiomatic phrases, university admissions interview prep, technical discussion vocabulary, and Goethe-Zertifikat B2 / TestDaF full-length mock drills.
